# Recall diagnostic: what the positions a high-volume recall closes earn

_Generated by `python scripts/claims/recall_diagnostic.py run --workers 8` from 474 cohorts (the full sample, July 1963 to December 2002), cohorts read from the run directory. Deterministic: no random number is drawn._

Claim 20 (b) (GGR 2006, p. 824): "The profits decline slightly by 4-13 bp per month, yet they remain large and positive." The paragraph reads on: "The results in Panel B can be interpreted as an estimate of the opportunity cost of short recalls." Twelve recall readings have measured that change (REVIEW_NOTES section 74) and every one of them raises our fully invested series; none has measured what the closed position itself would have earned. That is the quantity this page measures, on the no-recall path.

## 0. Read this first

The positions the R1 rule would close earn **+1.89 bp per day** after the recall day as a leg-spread return (+39.8 bp per month of position life; 59.6% of positions finish the window in the black) and -1.41 bp per day as the trade's own dollar payoff (-29.5 bp per month; 59.4% positive). The averages run over 959,560 recalled positions and 46,330,860 post-recall position-days of the no-recall run. The cells agree in sign: the leg-spread mean is positive in 4 of 4 portfolios and 20 of 20 portfolio-era cells.

**The pre-registered rule takes the positive branch**: a recall forgoes a real profit, which is the paper's own reading of the panel, so what remains is a comparison of size with the paper's 4-13 bp. Section 5 applies the rule and states the verdict; section 6 holds the proposed `CLAIMS.md` wording.

## 1. The rule that is being diagnosed

The reading is R1 of REVIEW_NOTES section 74, the one that ships (`--table9b-cache vol_common_recalls_r1`): a high-volume day on the short leg's volumes closes the pair at that day's close, also under the one-day wait. The flag is the paper's own definition (p. 824): "High-volume days are defined as days on which daily volume exceeds average daily volume over the 18 months (both split-adjusted) by more than one standard deviation." The implementation is the shipped one -- `project.recalls` for the threshold, `project.models` for the close -- on the volume window and cohort calendar of `table9b_recalls.build_cohort`.

Three conventions, each this page's reading and none a choice of parameter:

1. **A position is one open episode of the no-recall run.** The enumeration runs on the no-recall path (`recall=None`), so a pair that converges and re-diverges later is two positions; the rule is asked which of those positions it would have closed, and when. The recall run's own trade log is not the object of study: its re-entry episodes replace the closed ones and would hide the counterfactual.
2. **The recall day is the first flagged day after the open.** The engine tests the flag from the day after a position opens, so a flag on the open day itself closes nothing; R1 closes at the flagged day's own close, so no further day is needed.
3. **The position's return is the paper's per-trade return.** Each trade holds $1 long and $1 short, bought and held (`evaluation.py`, eqs. 2-3), so its daily return is `r_long - r_short` and its return over the post-recall window is `prod(1 + r_long) - prod(1 + r_short)` -- the trade's own payoff, which the engine logs as `payoff = L - S` at the natural close. The compounded figure is the one the paper's monthly means are built from; the per-day figure is the decision statistic.

A position is attributed to the era of the cohort that formed it. The completed run's monthly rows in section 4 are attributed by the month of the observation, the way the era census buckets a monthly series; the two differ only for cohorts that straddle an era boundary.

## 5. The decision rule, applied

Pre-registered in the contract, before the run: **if the post-recall-day return is negative on average, no closing rule can produce the paper's 4-13 bp decline on fully invested capital under our pinned definition** (fully invested = per open position, REVIEW_NOTES section 33), and claim 20 (b) is settled by the data as "partly, with the sign explained"; **if it is positive**, quantify the bp effect a recall rule would need and compare it with 4-13 bp.

The decision statistic, the all-pairs all-eras pooled mean post-recall-day return, is +1.893 bp per day (+39.8 bp per month of position life, 59.6% of positions finishing the window in the black). Read as the trade's own dollar payoff (`L - S`, the quantity the committed row is built from) the same positions earn -1.407 bp per day (-29.5 bp per month). Both readings cover 959,560 recalled positions and 46,330,860 post-recall position-days.

**The branch is positive: the return reading is positive.** A recall forgoes a real profit, the direction of the paper's own reading of the panel ("an estimate of the opportunity cost of short recalls"). Read as dollar payoffs the same positions are -1.41 bp per day, and the two readings differ because eq. (3) weights each leg by its accumulated value: a pair whose short leg has grown carries more dollars in the short leg than in the long one. What is left to compare is size, in the rows below.

The exact counterfactuals of section 4 are the undecorated test of claim 20 (b): they close the same positions in the same book and report the change of our own two rows, reading minus baseline. On committed capital the closing moves the row by -15.3 / -23.3 / -24.5 / -4.9 bp (the paper's sign in 4 of 4 portfolios, inside its 4-13 bp band in 1 of 4); on the pinned fully invested row by +18.6 / +45.6 / +34.9 / +87.7 bp (a rise in 4 of 4). R1's own finished run, which also re-enters on a new divergence, moves the same two rows by -11.0 / -17.2 / -19.5 / +1.2 committed and +5.5 / +20.6 / +20.2 / +62.0 fully invested (all eras).

**Verdict: claim 20 (b) is partly replicated, with the sign explained -- and the explanation is the row, not the positions.** The paper prints two rows, and its own changes are -12.6 / -4.1 / -13.0 / -13.0 bp fully invested (the 4-13 bp of the sentence) and -15.9 / -17.3 / -20.7 / -19.4 bp on committed capital. Our closing counterfactual reproduces the committed one in sign in 4 of 4 portfolios and at 1.0-1.4 times its size in three of them, and it raises the fully invested row in 4 of 4. The recalled positions are not losers that the rule throws out -- they keep earning +1.89 bp per day after the recall day, t = 5.9 -- so the recall really does cost the strategy the paper's sentence describes. What does not reproduce is that cost on the fully invested row: a closed position takes its accumulated long and short values out of both denominators of that value-weighted book (REVIEW_NOTES sections 33 and 54), which is why every reading of section 74 rises there. Claim 20 (b) therefore stays partly replicated: false on the fully invested row, and of the paper's sign and order on committed capital.

## 6. Proposed `CLAIMS.md` wording for claim 20 (b)

For the coordinator; `CLAIMS.md` on main is not edited by this branch.

> Claim 20 (b), "The profits decline slightly by 4-13 bp per month": **partly replicated, with the sign explained** (REVIEW_NOTES section 84). The positions the recall rule closes would have kept earning: the post-recall-day return over 959,560 recalled positions is +1.89 bp per day (+39.8 bp per month of position life), so the closure forgoes a real profit, the direction the paper's opportunity-cost sentence describes. What the closure does to each of our rows is measured directly: the committed row (the paper's own convention, sum pi / N) moves by -15.3 / -23.3 / -24.5 / -4.9 bp and the pinned fully invested row by +18.6 / +45.6 / +34.9 / +87.7 bp (all eras). The paper's own printed changes are -12.6 / -4.1 / -13.0 / -13.0 bp fully invested -- its 4-13 bp sentence -- and -15.9 / -17.3 / -20.7 / -19.4 bp on committed capital. The decline therefore has the paper's sign and order on committed capital, in 4 of 4 portfolios and inside its 4-13 bp band in 1 of 4; on the pinned fully invested row, where a closed position takes its accumulated values out of both denominators of the value-weighted book (REVIEW_NOTES sections 33 and 54), the sign is the other way and no closing rule can produce the paper's decline. The block is scored since SCORE_LOG row 22; this diagnostic moves no cell.

## 7. Reproduce

```sh
python scripts/claims/recall_diagnostic.py run --workers 8 \
    --out-root /tmp/R1/out \
    --report report/replication/claims/RECALL_DIAGNOSTIC.md \
    --csv report/replication/claims/recall_diagnostic.csv
```

Per-cohort outputs under `{out-root}/cohorts/YYYY-MM/`: `positions.parquet` (one row per recalled position -- pair rank, open, recall and natural close dates, the days after the recall, the summed and the compounded return), `paths.parquet` (the same positions' daily long-leg return, short-leg return and spread -- the return paths the enumeration asks for), `monthly.parquet` (the closing counterfactual's base and drop series) and `summary.json` (the controls and the per-portfolio counts). The `--stride 24` smoke run builds 20 cohorts and adds `--validate`, which reruns the engine's own recall run and checks every predicted close.

The shared caches `vol_common`, `vol_common_recalls` and `vol_common_recalls_r1` are only read.
